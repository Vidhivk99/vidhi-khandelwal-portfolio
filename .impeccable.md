## Design Context

### Users
Hiring managers, technical recruiters, and engineers evaluating Vidhi for AI, data, and software engineering roles. They need to understand project ownership, technical depth, and outcomes quickly without reading an activity log.

### Brand Personality
Focused, precise, and quietly confident. The work should feel substantial and inspectable without sounding self-promotional or turning implementation evidence into the main story.

### Aesthetic Direction
A project-led engineering portfolio with an editorial case-study rhythm. Use a light, high-contrast canvas, strong typography, real product imagery, and varied project layouts. Avoid blog-like feeds, contribution dashboards, PR-centric framing, and decorative technical jargon.

### Design Principles
- Projects are the primary navigation and the primary content.
- Lead with the problem, the system, and the result; keep proof subordinate.
- Show the actual product or project output whenever possible.
- Keep experience concise so it supports rather than competes with the work.
- Make the first project deep enough to demonstrate process and judgment.
